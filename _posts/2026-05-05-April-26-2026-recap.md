title: "April 26, 2026 recap"
---

Dear Cleveland fountain pen enthusiasts,

Thanks for a great meeting on April 26!  We enjoyed attendance of over 20
and shared outstanding discussion on our pens, inks and paper.
Interestingly, I noticed both modern and vintage pens in more or less equal
numbers.  I’ll outline some of the highlights of the meeting here, but that
said, everyone’s presence and conversation and friendship made the meeting
a real joy.

Joseph, a brand-new member, generously brought in a giveaway on his very
first visit!  He contributed a pen cleaning and tuning kit donated by
Fountain Pen Revolution.  Our thanks to Joseph and to Kevin from Fountain
Pen Revolution for that gift, and congratulations to Jonathan on winning
it!  I bet everyone has Platinum pens and inks on their minds now. 😁  Also,
David brought in an interesting display of Pilot nibs and shared
information from nib expert Richard Binder on general nib design and
function.

I hope and trust everyone enjoyed the afternoon.  I’m interested in
feedback from all attendees on our meetings.  What works well?  Where could
we improve?  Please let me know!

One topic of discussion was the Ohio Pen Show, typically scheduled for the
last weekend in October in Columbus.  It is unfortunately not in the works
this year, although there are hopes that it may be rescheduled to another
date.  The show is one of the highlights of autumn for me, so if I get any
news about it, I will certainly update you all.  For what it’s worth, the
show website is www.theohiopenshow.com.  I did have a very happy dream last
night that I was at the show and discovered Galen Leather’s table there, so
I will take that as a good sign!  Fingers crossed—I hope we get some good
news.  At the very least, we have our own pen club meetups, Fountain Pen
Day on the first Friday of November, and the Pelikan Hub in the fall, date
and time TBA.

The next meeting of the Cleveland Pen Club will be on July 26, 2026, at
1:30PM – 3:30PM at the Cuyahoga County Public Library, Independence Branch,
6361 Selig Dr, Independence, OH 44131.  We meet quarterly on the fourth
Sundays in January, April, July and October.  Please join us if you can,
and invite any locals you know who are interested in the fountain pen
hobby.

Before I close, I’ll share a few useful resources.

Last, a quote I found online, credited to Jamil Zaki:  Artificial
intelligence has already allowed us to become cognitively sedentary, and
that will only increase over time.  But just as we must be responsible for
our bodies in an era of office work, we must now keep our minds active.
Writing is a gymnasium for human thought we need now more than ever.

If you have any questions, suggestions, or ideas to share in advance of
the next meeting, please reach out.  Otherwise, enjoy your pens, inks and
paper, and spread the word about our club!  I look forward to our next
gathering.

--Michelle
