Hi Cleveland pen friends,

Just a reminder of our next Cleveland Pen Club meetup on Sunday, January
25, 2026!  No RSVP is needed.  Join us at 1:30-3:30 PM at the Cuyahoga
County Public Library – Independence Branch, 6361 Selig Dr., Independence,
OH.  We meet in the large meeting room to the left of the front entrance.
Bring your favorite pens, inks, and paper, fountain pen related items for
the swap table if you like, and a snack to share if you like.  I’ve planned
a couple surprises including a second game and special giveaway!  And there
will still be plenty of time for organic conversation around our favorite
pens, inks and paper.

As I sit here watching the endless snow out my back window, I sincerely
hope the weather cooperates on January 25 so we can gather and enjoy our
wonderful hobby.  Contact me if you have any questions.  I look forward to
seeing everyone!

--Michelle
