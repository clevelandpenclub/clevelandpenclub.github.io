Thank you and Howard for these fascinating bits of fountain pen history. I
think you need to caution members that these are not the best repair
techniques today. We've advanced quite a bit since what Father Terry
advocated 30 or 40 years ago. If people are seriously interested in vintage
pen repair, you should recommend the 4th edition of "Pen Repair" by
Marshall and Oldfield. They're based in the UK and compiled the best
practices of restorations today. Richard Binder's website, richardspens.com,
also has repair tutorials that are correct and up-to-date. Back when Father
Terry was selling parts and doing repairs, there were no alternative parts
or techniques available so he did what he had to do to get the pens to
work. Now there are better ways that aren't potentially damaging to pens.

Enjoy your pens, inks, and paper today!

--Michelle
