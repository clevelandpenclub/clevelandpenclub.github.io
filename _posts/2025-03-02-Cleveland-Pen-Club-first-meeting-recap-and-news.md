title: "Cleveland Pen Club first meeting recap and news"
---

Hello Cleveland Pen Club enthusiasts,

It’s been a minute!  The inaugural meeting of the Cleveland Pen Club on
January 26 was a great success.  Highlights for me personally were meeting
and re-meeting great people, and seeing some impressive pens in the wild
(I’m looking at you, Pilot Myu).  Around 20 people attended and many stayed
beyond the allotted time because it was SO much fun!

I think the consensus is that we all very much look forward to the next
one.  It will take place on April 27, 2025 at 1:30 p.m.  I have the same
meeting room booked at the Cuyahoga County Public Library Independence
Branch, 6361 Selig Dr, Independence, OH.  When you arrive, the meeting room
is to the left of the front doors.  Please put the meeting on your calendar
and feel free to share this invitation with other pen people you know.

Exciting news:  The Cleveland Pen Club now has its own website,
clevelandpen.club, and a new email address, clevelandpenclub@gmail.com.
This email address reaches me personally.  These are the ways to keep in
touch moving forward.  The website will be kept up-to-date with our meetup
schedule, any extracurricular activities we plan, and useful resources.

One such resource is Pen Collectors of [America](https://pencollectorsofamerica.org/.)  This great organization was
introduced at our January meeting, and all are encouraged to consider
membership.

Another resource is the 2025 pen show [schedule](https://www.wellappointeddesk.com/pen-show-schedule/.)  I plan to attend
the 2025 Ohio Pen Show later in the year and hope to be allowed to provide
a small printed announcement and sign-up sheet for our club somewhere at
the show.

If you have any questions, suggestions or comments, feel free to send them
my way.  Hope to see you on April 27!

--Michelle
