Good afternoon Cleveland fountain pen enthusiasts,

I just learned about an ink/washi swap taking place at Milan Public Library
on Monday, June 9 at 6:00 p.m.  Attendees can bring bottles of ink they are
interested in sharing, and the host, Christy, will provide sample vials and
syringes. The library is located at 19 East Church St, Milan, OH 44846.
The library phone number is (419) 499-4117.  Here is the link to register
for this event:
https://milanberlin.librarycalendar.com/event/planner-society-ink-washi-swap-2675

Here is a link to Christy’s ink share spreadsheet listing her current
[collection.](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/13Mx_vJjMih6_nRJnPU9aZuJekURBq-WKqP0jEwHNWC0/edit?gid=1517726679#gid=1517726679)

I hope some can attend this event which sounds like a lot of fun!
