Dear Cleveland fountain pen enthusiasts,

Thanks for a great meeting on October 25! We enjoyed outstanding
discussion on our pens, inks and paper. Everyone’s presence and
conversation and friendship gave us, as usual, a great afternoon.

Once again, Joseph generously donated a giveaway, a very nice
custom-engraved orange fountain pen from Fountain Pen Revolution. Katie
won the pen. Congrats Katie! Joseph then participated in our 20
questions. I hope you all enjoy that activity as much as I do.

The next meeting of the Cleveland Pen Club will be on January 24, 2027, at
1:30PM – 3:30PM. Same location: Cuyahoga County Public Library,
Independence Branch, 6361 Selig Dr, Independence, OH 44131. We meet
quarterly on the fourth Sundays in January, April, July and October. Plan
to join us, and invite others. We may open up even more club meetings, and
when plans are firm I will let everyone know.

Registration is open for the Ohio Pen Show, December 3-6, in Columbus at
https://theohiopenshow.com/. If you’re planning to stay overnight, I suggest contacting the hotel
asap to get the pen show discount rate. The
show has a new date and location this year, and it should be a good time.
I will definitely look forward to getting a room key that works! (IYKYK)
If you have never attended a pen show before, I encourage you to try it.
David Parker’s Pen Show 101
https://youtu.be/eJj1FP3YPYw?si=Htp6chEYrGIyBDWG video is a great
introduction to the pen show experience.

Fountain Pen Day 2026 is coming up very soon on November 6. Website is
https://www.fountainpenday.com/. There is a little confusion because the
sponsor section mentions 2025, but at any rate it’s a great list of places
to source pens. Speaking of which, I distributed a list of fountain pen
retailers at our meeting. I'm attaching it to this email. If you know a
business that should be on that list but is not, please let me know!

The announcement I am most excited about is that Edison Pen Company has
invited us for a facility tour! Edison Pen Company is located in Huron,
Ohio, about an hour’s drive west of the library where we meet. Owner Brian
Gray will take us through the creation of a custom pen from start to
finish, including tuning the nib to perfection. If you are interested in
this, I want to know what scheduling works best for you. Please complete
this Google form, Edison Tour Questionnaire
https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfh-jTNiLM2VsXJL5FkFXb7bk5Q09aR-VxVm5uLZp-jymsFMg/viewform?usp=header,
so we can pick the best date in January, February or March. Once I have
good data, I will announce the date and other details.

Thanks again, everyone, for making our club a lot of fun. If you have any
questions, suggestions, or ideas to share in advance of the next meeting,
please reach out. Otherwise, enjoy your pens, inks and paper, and spread
the word about our club! I look forward to our next gathering.

--Michelle
