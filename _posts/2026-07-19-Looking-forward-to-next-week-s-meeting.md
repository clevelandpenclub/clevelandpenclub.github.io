Hello Cleveland area fountain pen enthusiasts,

Here's your reminder of our meeting on Sunday, July 26 at 1:30 - 3:30PM.
We will meet at the Cuyahoga County Public Library Independence Branch,
6361 Selig Dr, Independence, OH 44131.  As a reminder, meetings are
quarterly on the fourth Sundays in January, April, July and October.  By
the way, I have decided to no longer bring bottled water to our pen
meetups.  The library offers a drinking fountain, and everyone is welcome
to bring their own water and snacks to share.  Mainly, bring favorite pens,
inks, and paper to show off, share, or trade.  By the way, no RSVPs are
needed for this club!

Please reach out separate from this email with any questions, suggestions,
or ideas.  Spread the word about our club to any local peeps who might be
interested in joining us.  Until then, stay well, and enjoy your pens,
inks, and paper.  Hope to see you Sunday!

--Michelle
