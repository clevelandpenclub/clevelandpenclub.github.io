Dear Cleveland pen friends,

As the year draws to a close, I want to send warm wishes to you all.  It is
a great pleasure to be a part of this community, and I am thankful.  Thank
you for the conversations, the show-and-tell moments, the pen and ink
recommendations,
and the generosity of spirit that have characterized our gatherings. From
shimmering inks to well-loved pens and favorite notebooks shared at our
meetups, it’s been a delight to enjoy this hobby with such a wonderful
group.

I hope your holiday celebrations are filled with rest and joy, the love of
family and friends, and a little extra time to write, reflect, and enjoy
the writing tools you love.  Here’s to fresh pages, inspiring inks, and
many happy pen-filled moments in the year ahead.  May your holidays and the
new year be joyful and bright.

--Michelle
