Hello Cleveland area fountain pen enthusiasts,

Here's your reminder of our meeting on Sunday, October 25, at 1:30 -
3:30PM. We will meet at the Cuyahoga County Public Library Independence
Branch, 6361 Selig Dr, Independence, OH 44131. As a reminder, meetings are
quarterly on the fourth Sundays in January, April, July and October.
Everyone is welcome to bring their own drinking water, and snacks to
share. Mainly, bring favorite pens, inks, and paper to show off, share, or
trade. Bring cordless table lamps and a loupe if you have them. By the
way, no RSVPs are needed for this club!

Please reach out with any questions, suggestions, or ideas. Spread the
word about our club to any local peeps who might be interested in joining
us. Until then, stay well, and enjoy your pens, inks, and paper. Hope to
see you Sunday!

--Michelle
