title: "October 26 Recap"
---

Hello Cleveland fountain pen enthusiasts!!

Autumn and fountain pen season are both upon us—which I say very happily.
This weekend was delightful with the annual Pelikan Hub on Friday night and
our own Cleveland Pen Club meetup on Sunday afternoon.  Fourteen people
attended the pen club, including some newcomers.  The pens and inks were
beautiful and the discussion engaging, as we’ve come to expect.  Eric was a
good sport and participated in the 20 Questions game I am really enjoying.
It draws everyone in and sparks great discussion.  Many received extra
gifts of ink from the Pelikan Hub, beautiful gift boxes containing a
postcard, a notepad, and Pelikan’s current ink of the year, Pelikan
Edelstein Apricot Achat.  If you missed the Hub or want more information on
it, visit www.pelikan-hubs.com.

Two coming events.  First, the Ohio Pen Show is this weekend, October 30 –
November 2 at the Crown Plaza Columbus North, 6500 Doubletree Avenue,
Columbus, Ohio.  If you are planning to attend and stay overnight, I
suggest making your reservation as soon as possible.  For more information,
visit www.theohiopenshow.com.  Look for me there beginning Friday
afternoon.  Our club doesn't have a table, but I will be present at the
show distributing our new postcards, attached!

Second, Fountain Pen Day is coming up on November 7.  This has become a
tradition on the first Friday of November each year.  Look for a lot of
buzz online as well as specials and discounts from fountain pen retailers.

The next fountain pen show in the U.S. is the Philly Pen Show scheduled for
January 16-18, 2026, followed by the LA show Feb 19–22 and
Baltimore-Washington Mar 6–8.  A more complete list is available [at](https://www.wellappointeddesk.com/pen-show-schedule/.)  I have attended the
Ohio Pen Show for a few years running and enjoy it thoroughly.  If you have
never attended a pen show, I highly recommend it.  If you have never been,
here is a helpful video on what to know before you [go.](https://youtu.be/eJj1FP3YPYw?si=xcLSHhSpH-4tc9sM)

The next meeting of the Cleveland Pen Club will be January 25, 2026, at
1:30PM – 3:30PM at the Cuyahoga County Public Library, Independence Branch,
6361 Selig Dr, Independence, OH 44131.  We meet quarterly on the fourth
Sundays in January, April, July and October.

If you have any questions, suggestions, or ideas to share in advance of our
January meeting, please reach out.  Otherwise, enjoy your pens, inks and
paper, and spread the word about our club!  I look forward to our next
gathering.

--Michelle
