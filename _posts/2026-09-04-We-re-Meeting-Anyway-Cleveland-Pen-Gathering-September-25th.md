title: "We're Meeting Anyway — Cleveland Pen Gathering, September 25th"
---

Cleveland fountain pen enthusiasts,

Cleveland won't have an official Pelikan Hub this year, as you may have
heard. Good news: we're getting together anyway, thanks to the efforts of
Dan Szekeres. Obviously we'll miss out on the Pelikan-sponsored swag, but
it's about the people and the pens after all. So consider this your
invitation to an unofficial Cleveland pen meetup:

When: Friday, September 25th, 6pm–9pm (same approximate time as a Pelikan
Hub)

Where: Panera Bread, 5090 Tiedeman Rd, Brooklyn, OH 44144
[Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/5090+Tiedeman+Rd,+Brooklyn,+OH+44144)

RSVP [HERE](https://pelikanthubcleveland.rsvpify.com/) but walk-ins will
also be welcome!

There is no cost, but you are strongly encouraged to grab a bite or a drink
while we're there, as supporting the venue is what makes this meeting spot
available to us. Bring your favorite pens, inks, and paper to show off,
and feel free to bring a friend who's curious about the hobby.

For questions, contact Dan at djszekeres@gmail.com. Spread the word to
your local fountain pen friends, and hope to see you!

--Michelle
