Hello Cleveland Pen Club enthusiasts,

Happy birthday!  Cleveland Pen Club is now a year old.  Our rescheduled
meeting on February 1 was a little smaller but, as always, tons of fun.
The meeting had been scheduled for January 25, but that was the weekend of
the awful snowstorm and the library didn’t even open!  When we did meet at
our alternate location, the Brooklyn branch of the Cuyahoga County Public
Library, we enjoyed our pen, ink, and paper adventures as usual, with a
surprise or two.  One notable surprise was the delicious homemade molasses
cookies Barbie brought in.  Thanks again Barbie!

The second surprise was a really great raffle item.  Back in early November
at the Ohio Pen Show, I stopped at the table of Paladin Pen Company and met
the owner, Matt Eichenfels.  Matt generously gave me a gift to raffle off
at our club!  I conducted the raffle by leading a game of Fountain Pen
Bingo during our 20 questions segment.  Patrick and Jonathan both
participated in the 20 questions – thanks guys!  Daniel won the Paladin
gift – congrats Daniel!  He received a beautiful glass pen and a Paladin
Pen Company sticker.  I trust that everyone enjoyed the Bingo game, and I
saved it to use again sometime.

Howard asked me to share with you all the attached article.  During the
meeting he also highly recommended an Italian restaurant on Memphis Avenue
called Aldo’s.  Their website is www.aldoscleveland.com.

Next time, we will be back at the normal location, Cuyahoga County Public
Library, Independence Branch, 6361 Selig Dr, Independence, OH 44131, on
 Sunday, April 26 at 1:30 - 3:30PM.  We meet quarterly on the fourth
Sundays in January, April, July and October.  By the way, I have decided to
no longer bring bottled water to our pen meetups.  It is a lot of weight
for me to drag around, the library offers a drinking fountain, and everyone
is welcome to bring their own water anyway.

If you have any questions, suggestions, or ideas to share in advance of our
next meeting, please reach out.  I hope that in the meantime some of you
get to attend a pen show or make a new fountain pen purchase.  In fact, if
any of that happens for you in the coming months, please share about it
when we meet!

I look forward to our next gathering.  Spread the word about our club to
any local peeps who might be interested in joining us.  Until then, stay
well, and enjoy your pens, inks, and paper.

--Michelle
