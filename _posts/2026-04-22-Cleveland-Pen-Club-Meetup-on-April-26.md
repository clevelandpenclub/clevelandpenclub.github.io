title: "Cleveland Pen Club Meetup on April 26!"
---

Hello Cleveland area fountain pen enthusiasts,

Here's your reminder of our meeting on Sunday, April 26 at 1:30 - 3:30PM.
We will be back at the normal location, Cuyahoga County Public Library
Independence Branch, 6361 Selig Dr, Independence, OH 44131.  As a reminder,
meetings are quarterly on the fourth Sundays in January, April, July and
October.  By the way, I have decided to no longer bring bottled water to
our pen meetups.  It is a lot of weight for me to drag around and the
library offers a drinking fountain.  Everyone is welcome to bring their own
water and snacks to share.  Mainly, bring favorite pens, inks, and paper to
show off, share, or trade.  By the way, no RSVPs are needed for this club!

I have one or two simple ideas for our meeting which I hope you all will
enjoy.  Meanwhile, please reach out with any questions, suggestions, or
ideas.  Spread the word about our club to any local peeps who might be
interested in joining us.  Until then, stay well, and enjoy your pens,
inks, and paper.  Hope to see you Sunday!

--Michelle
